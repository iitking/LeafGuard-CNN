Pathogen & Condition Overview

Pathogen: Alternaria solani (Fungus)

Foliar disease characterized by concentric target-board rings on older foliage, yellow halos, and defoliation.

  • Dark brown spots with concentric target-board rings.
  • Yellowing (chlorosis) surrounding the spots.
Eco-Friendly & Non-Toxic: Safe for organic gardens, beneficial pollinators, and edible produce.
  • Apply neem oil or sulfur-based organic fungicides.
Commercial Fungicide Notice: Always follow local regulatory guidelines, application rates, and pre-harvest intervals.
  • Apply Chlorothalonil or Mancozeb sprays.
  • Rotate tomato crops every 2-3 years away from solanaceous plants.
